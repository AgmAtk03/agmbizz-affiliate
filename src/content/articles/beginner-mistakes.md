Most wasted spend in Your Niche (once a real niche exists) will come from **skipping the boring work**.

This stub lists the mistakes we expect to document for real. None of these are product reviews.

## Buying before defining the job

If you cannot explain the outcome in a sentence, a “best” list is just advertising. Start with [how to evaluate tools](/guides/getting-started/evaluate-tools/).

## Copying someone else’s stack

A creator’s setup is a case study, not a specification. Your constraints — budget, time, skill — are the spec.

## Treating one article as the whole site

Google and readers both do better when a topic is a **cluster**. This page should stay a supporting note under [Getting Started in Your Niche](/guides/getting-started/), not a doorway clone of the buying guides.

## Ignoring the disclosure

If a page is trying to get you to buy, it should say so in plain language. Ours does, sitewide. Read the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
