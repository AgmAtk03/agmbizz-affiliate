Print this, or duplicate it into your notes. Replace every “Your Niche” line when the category is real.

## Before you search

- [ ] Write the job in one sentence
- [ ] Write the constraint (budget, time, skill, space)
- [ ] Write what “done” looks like in 30 days
- [ ] Decide whether you need a tool at all

## While you research

- [ ] Collect official docs, not just roundups
- [ ] Note what each source was paid to say
- [ ] Separate must-have from nice-to-have
- [ ] Keep a list of questions vendors have not answered

## Before you buy

- [ ] Re-read [how to evaluate tools](/guides/getting-started/evaluate-tools/)
- [ ] Compare at least two options with the [buying criteria](/guides/buying-guides/buying-criteria/)
- [ ] Check return windows and data export
- [ ] Read this site’s [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) so you know how we get paid

This checklist lives under [Getting Started in Your Niche](/guides/getting-started/). It should stay useful even if every product on the buying-guide pillar changes.
