## Criteria before the link

Score a Your Niche purchase against criteria you wrote down, not against a homepage banner.

1. **Job fit** — Does it do the sentence you wrote on the [evaluation page](/guides/getting-started/evaluate-tools/)?
2. **Total cost** — Include the parts the landing page leaves in a footnote.
3. **Reversibility** — Returns, exports, and lock-in.
4. **Evidence** — What you personally verified versus what a roundup repeated.

Only after that should a commercial link appear.

## Sample offer (placeholder)

When you publish a real offer, mark it like this:
<SponsoredLink href="https://example.com/go/sample-niche-kit" class="font-semibold" label="Sample Niche Kit — example.com placeholder" />.
That anchor is a real `<a>` in the built HTML with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`. There is no redirect cloak and no JavaScript required to emit it.
