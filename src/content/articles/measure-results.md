If you cannot measure the job, you will buy decorations.

This stub keeps measurement **separate from shopping** so the playbook cluster stays useful.

## Pick two numbers

Examples to replace later:

- A leading indicator you can see weekly (sessions completed, units shipped, hours saved)
- A lagging indicator you can see monthly (cost per outcome, error rate, revenue)

Write them next to the job sentence from [how to evaluate tools](/guides/getting-started/evaluate-tools/).

## Write the review date

A metric without a calendar is a mood. Put the date on the brief in the [practical workflow](/guides/how-to-playbooks/practical-workflow/).

## Do not let a vendor dashboard choose the story

Merchant analytics exist to retain you. Keep an independent note — a spreadsheet is enough.
