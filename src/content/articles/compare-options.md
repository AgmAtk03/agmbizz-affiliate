This is a **commercial page**. If we later recommend a real product, the link will be marked sponsored. Until then, the names below are labeled samples.

## Compare the job, then the SKU

Carry the job sentence from [how to evaluate tools](/guides/getting-started/evaluate-tools/) into this table. Do not start from a merchant’s category page.

| Sample option | Best for (stub) | Watch-out (stub) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample Brand A Starter | Beginners documenting a first workflow | Missing export path in the fictional spec |
| Sample Niche Kit | Readers who want a bundled “good enough” set | Bundle may include items you will not use |
| Sample Brand B Pro | Teams that already know the job | Higher cost that is not automatically “better” |

Those rows are **not reviews**. When the niche is real, rewrite them from primary research or clearly labeled second-hand sources.

## Where a sponsored link belongs

When you are ready to send a reader to a merchant, use the component — never a raw untagged anchor. The [sample buying criteria](/guides/buying-guides/buying-criteria/) page shows that pattern in MDX.
