Playbooks should teach a reader **how to do the work**, not how to click through a maze of thin pages.

This pillar is the home for workflows, checklists, and measurement notes. Child articles should be independently useful and should link back here so Google (and humans) can see the cluster.

When the niche is chosen, keep the shape:

- One job per article
- Internal links to the related buying guide when a tool is actually needed
- No doorway variations of the same outline
