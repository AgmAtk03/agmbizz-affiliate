Buying guides are where **commercial intent** lives. That is also where affiliate compliance is easiest to get wrong.

This scaffold shows the pattern we want:

1. Criteria first, SKUs second
2. Named sample offers only as **clearly labeled stubs**
3. Outbound commercial links through `SponsoredLink`
4. A persistent disclosure above the fold

When you swap in a real niche, replace the sample brands. Do not invent ratings, “we tested 47 products,” or review scores you cannot defend.
