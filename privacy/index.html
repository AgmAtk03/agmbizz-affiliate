Privacy policy

Last updated September 6, 2026

AgmBizz is a statically generated website. We do not ship a user database or accounts. This policy describes the small amount of information that can still move through the scaffold.

Information you send us

The email capture and contact forms post to whatever endpoint you set inPUBLIC_FORM_ACTION (for example Formspree), or they fall back to mailto. That provider’s privacy terms apply to addresses and messages. We do not store submissions in this repository.

Server and platform logs

If you host on Vercel or another CDN, the platform may log IP addresses, user agents, and request paths for security and operations. Review that host’s data processing terms.

Cookies and analytics

This scaffold does not set analytics cookies. If you add a script later, update this page and give readers a real choice where the law requires one.

Affiliate cookies

Clicking a sponsored link may cause a merchant or network to set their own cookies so a commission can be attributed. Those cookies are controlled by the destination, not byAgmBizz.
